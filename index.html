KWTool

Simple keyword research, without the noise.

KWTool helps you find keyword ideas and check how often people search for them, so you can pick topics worth writing about and products worth trying.

We are building the first version now. It will let you type in a word or phrase, see related keyword ideas, and see estimated monthly search volume.

What KWTool does

  • Find related keyword ideas for a topic.
  • Show estimated search volume for each keyword.
  • Help you compare keywords before you write or sell.
